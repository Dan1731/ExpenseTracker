## Vision Statement
Penny's vision is to give college students the power to take control of their finances by making it
easier to understand, manage, and improve their spending and debt. Penny combines debt and expense
management with social interaction to make building healthy money habits a fun, fresh experience.
***
## Motivation
Financial struggles are a common theme among college students. Managing classes, social life, and oftentimes
work can make it hard to manage finances. Penny aims to solve this issue by providing an engaging, intuitive,
purpose-built platform that makes managing finances and building healthy money habits fun.
***
## Risks to completion
- **Scheudling:** The team is going to have to rely mainly on asynchronous communcation with the exception
of the weekly Scrum meeting via Zoom.
- **Learning Curve w/ New Technologies:** Members of the team may need to learn new technologies (languages, frameworks, etc.)
to bring the project to completion.
- **Scope Creep:** There is a risk of committing to implementing too many features, which could lead to an incomplete project
at the end of the semester.
***
## Mitigation Strategy
- **Scheduling:** To overcome the issues that come along with asynchronous communication the team will utilize asynchronous
communication tools and ensure that the weekly meetings are productive. The team may also schedule one-off meeting if they
are deemed necessary.
- **Learning Curve:** The team plans to lean on each member's strengths when necessary to ensure that features are being completed
on schedule.
- **Scope Creep:** Keeping the MVP of the application simple to start will ensure that there is a functional product by the end of
the semester. Once the MVP is up and running, the team can work on implementing additional features.
***
## Development Method
The team plans to apply an Agile approach to development. The team will have short weekly meetings where they plan sprints,
communicate potential blockers, keep the project on track.
